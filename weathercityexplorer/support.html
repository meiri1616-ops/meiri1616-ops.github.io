Weather City Explorer — Support

Weather City Explorer shows live weather for cities as illustrated, miniature landmark scenes. The scenes follow the real weather and time of day, and the app helps you plan photos with golden-hour and blue-hour times.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the weather come from?

Weather data is provided by Apple Weather (WeatherKit). The Apple Weather attribution and a link to its data sources are at the bottom of the Forecast screen: tap the landmark and temperature bar, then scroll to the end.

How do I change city?

Tap the city name at the top left. Pick an illustrated city, search for any city in the world, or tap Use Current Location.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only when you tap Use Current Location. The app makes a single, approximate lookup to find your city and its weather. It does not track you in the background. You can change this in Settings › Weather City Explorer › Location.

What are the bell reminders?

Tap the bell capsule on a landmark to get a local notification before a good golden hour or blue hour there. Reminders last 3 days and you can turn them off anytime. Change the times and quiet hours from the bell icon in the city list. If notifications don't arrive, check Settings › Notifications › Weather City Explorer.

How do I save a scene?

Long-press the scene to save it to your Photos. The app only asks to add photos; it cannot see your library.

What does the parrot do?

The parrot is your guide. It shares short, pre-written tips about the weather and the view. It isn't a chatbot and doesn't send anything you do anywhere.

The weather looks wrong or won't load

Check your internet connection, then reopen the app. If the problem continues, email us with the city name and time.

Is my data shared?

No account, no ads, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
