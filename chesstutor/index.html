Chess Tutor: AI

Chess Tutor: AI is a free teaching app for international chess. It does not only play a game. After every move, numbered arrows replay the AI’s thinking steps. The app also shows candidate moves and a win-chance bar.

You play locally against Stockfish, at strengths from beginner through master. Also included: hints, undo, a think drawer, a choice of White, Black, or random, a rules reference, and move sounds and haptics. There is no user account and no sign-in. Gameplay and the engine run on the device, so no network connection is required to play.
