Chinese chess AI tutor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Chinese chess AI tutor ("the app") is Xiangqi (Chinese chess) for iPhone and iPad. You play against a local, on-device AI. This policy explains what the app uses, what it does not use, and how to contact us.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • The app has no servers of its own and does not use a cloud chess engine.
  • Win-rate estimates, move arrows, and the AI thinking-steps list are computed on your device.
  • Preferences such as difficulty, pace, and side stay on your device and are removed if you delete the app.
  • We do not sell or share personal data or track you across apps or websites.

Information the app uses

On-device play and analysis

The app computes the following on your device from the engine’s real search:

  • current-position win-rate estimates;
  • last-move arrows;
  • forecast arrows;
  • the AI thinking-steps list (piece icons and from–to notation).

These results are not sent to a server. There is no cloud chess engine.

Data stored on your device

User preferences such as difficulty, pace, and side stay on the device. They are removed if you delete the app. The app does not upload them, because it has no servers of its own.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, or track users across apps or websites.
  • We do not operate servers or a cloud chess engine for this app.

Third-party services

The app does not send gameplay, analysis, or preferences to a third-party service. It has no servers of its own, does not use a cloud chess engine, and does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can play without creating an account or signing in.
  • Deleting the app removes the preferences it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: meiri1616@gmail.com

Developer: Chulei Yang (杨出类). If you email us, we use your address only to respond. We do not use it for marketing or any other purpose.

Chinese chess AI tutor — 隐私政策

简要说明

  • 无账号、无登录、无广告、无分析、无跟踪。
  • 本应用没有自己的服务器，也不使用云端棋力引擎。
  • 胜率估计、着法箭头和 AI 思考步骤列表都在您的设备上计算。

本应用使用的信息

  • AI 思考步骤列表（棋子图标和起止记谱）。

这些结果不会发送到服务器。没有云端棋力引擎。

第三方服务

本应用不会将对局、分析或偏好发送给第三方服务。它没有自己的服务器，不使用云端棋力引擎，也不包含广告、分析或崩溃报告 SDK。

儿童

本应用适合所有年龄，且不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。
