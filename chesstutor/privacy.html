Chess Tutor: AI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Chess Tutor: AI (“the app”) is a free teaching app for international chess. This policy states what the app does with personal data.

Summary

  • The app does not collect personal data. This matches the App Store privacy declaration “Data Not Collected.”
  • There is no user account and no sign-in.
  • The app does not track you, and it does not sell data.
  • Gameplay and the engine run on the device. No network connection is required to play.

Personal data

The app does not collect personal data. It does not ask you to create an account or sign in. It does not track you. It does not sell data.

Play stays on the device. You play locally against Stockfish, at strengths from beginner through master. After every move, numbered arrows replay the AI’s thinking steps on the device, together with candidate moves and a win-chance bar. Hints, undo, the think drawer, the choice of White, Black, or random, the rules reference, and move sounds and haptics are part of that on-device play. None of this requires a network connection.

Contact by email

Email to meiri1616@gmail.com is the only contact method. If you choose to write, use that address for questions about the app, the thinking-steps display, or gameplay. The message you send is how you reach the developer, Chulei Yang. The app itself still does not collect personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated.

Chess Tutor: AI — 隐私政策

生效日期：2026年11月26日

Chess Tutor: AI（“本应用”）是一款免费的国际象棋教学应用。本政策说明本应用如何对待个人数据。

概要

  • 本应用不收集个人数据。这与 App Store 隐私声明“Data Not Collected”（未收集数据）一致。
  • 没有用户账号，也无需登录。
  • 本应用不跟踪您，也不出售数据。
  • 对局和引擎都在设备上运行。下棋不需要网络连接。

个人数据

本应用不收集个人数据。它不要求您创建账号或登录，不跟踪您，也不出售数据。

对局留在设备上。您在本地与 Stockfish 对弈，强度从初学者到大师。每走一步之后，带编号的箭头会在设备上回放 AI 的思考步骤，并一同显示候选着法与获胜机会条。提示、悔棋、思考抽屉、执白、执黑或随机、规则参考，以及走子音效和触感反馈，都属于这种在设备上的对局。这些都不需要网络连接。
