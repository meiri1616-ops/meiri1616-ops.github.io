Chinese chess AI tutor — Support

Chinese chess AI tutor is Xiangqi (Chinese chess) for iPhone and iPad. You play against a local, on-device AI. There is no cloud chess engine, no account or sign-in, and no ads. The app has no servers of its own.

Current-position win-rate estimates, last-move arrows, forecast arrows, and the AI thinking-steps list (piece icons and from–to notation) are computed on the device from the engine’s real search.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Open the app and play Xiangqi against the on-device AI. No account or sign-in is required. You can set difficulty, pace, and which side you play. Those preferences stay on your device.

Do I need an account?

No. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

How do I view the AI thinking steps?

The app shows an AI thinking-steps list computed on the device from the engine’s real search. Each step uses piece icons and from–to notation. Current-position win-rate estimates, last-move arrows, and forecast arrows are calculated on the device the same way. None of this is sent to a cloud chess engine.

How do difficulties work?

You can set difficulty, pace, and which side you play. Those preferences stay on your device and are removed if you delete the app. Changing them does not send your game to a server. The opponent is the local, on-device AI.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Chinese chess AI tutor is for iPhone and iPad.

How do I reach support?

Email meiri1616@gmail.com. We use that address only to respond to you. See also the Privacy Policy.
