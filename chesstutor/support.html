Chess Tutor: AI — Support

Chess Tutor: AI is a free teaching app for international chess. You play locally against Stockfish, at strengths from beginner through master. Gameplay and the engine run on the device. No network connection is required to play. There is no user account and no sign-in.

The app does not only play. After every move, numbered arrows replay the AI’s thinking steps. It also shows candidate moves and a win-chance bar. While you play you can use hints, undo, and a think drawer, choose White, Black, or random, open a rules reference, and use move sounds and haptics.

Contact

Email is the only way to reach support. For questions about the app, the thinking-steps display, or gameplay, write to meiri1616@gmail.com.

Chess Tutor: AI — 支持

Chess Tutor: AI 是一款免费的国际象棋教学应用。您在本地与 Stockfish 对弈，强度从初学者到大师。对局和引擎都在设备上运行，下棋不需要网络连接。没有用户账号，也无需登录。

本应用不只是对局。每走一步之后，带编号的箭头会回放 AI 的思考步骤，并显示候选着法与获胜机会条。对局时可以使用提示、悔棋和思考抽屉，选择执白、执黑或随机，打开规则参考，并使用走子音效和触感反馈。
