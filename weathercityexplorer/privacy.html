Weather City Explorer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Weather City Explorer ("the app") shows the weather for cities as illustrated, miniature scenes. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • The app has no servers of its own. It stores nothing about you on servers we control.
  • Your location is used only when you tap Use Current Location, and only to find your nearby city and its weather.
  • Everything else you set up (settings, followed landmarks, reminders) stays on your device.

Information the app uses

Location (only if you choose)

When you tap Use Current Location, iOS asks for "While Using the App" permission. If you allow it, the app asks for a single, approximate (about 1 km accuracy) position. It does not track your location continuously or in the background.

That position is sent to:

  • Apple (WeatherKit and Apple's geocoding service) to get the weather and the name of your city;
  • BigDataCloud (api.bigdatacloud.net), only as a backup if Apple's service cannot name your city. The app sends the latitude and longitude only, with no identifiers.

The app does not keep a history of your locations.

City search

When you search for a city, your search text is sent to Apple's geocoding service (MapKit) to find matching places. It is not stored by us.

Weather data

Weather comes from Apple WeatherKit for the cities you view. Requests include the city's coordinates. They do not include your name or any account identifier.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device, using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults):

  • landmarks you follow for photo reminders, and your reminder preferences;
  • whether you have already seen certain explanation screens.

This data never leaves your device through the app. It is removed when you delete the app.

Notifications

If you turn on photo reminders (for example, "golden hour" or "blue hour" alerts), the app asks for permission to send notifications. These are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification service or device token is used. The app may refresh reminder times in the background using iOS Background App Refresh.

Photos

If you choose to save a scene image, the app asks for permission to add photos to your library. It cannot read or browse your existing photos.

Spoken narration

Some scenes include spoken narration. This is generated on your device by iOS text-to-speech. No audio is recorded and the microphone is not used.

The parrot guide

The parrot's tips are pre-written lines bundled inside the app. Nothing you do is sent to an AI service.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or photos.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data, or use it to track you across apps or websites.

Third-party services

  • Apple WeatherKit, MapKit and geocoding — Apple Privacy Policy
  • BigDataCloud (location-name backup only) — BigDataCloud Privacy Policy

These providers process requests under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke location, notification, and photo permissions at any time in Settings › Weather City Explorer. The app keeps working; you can pick cities manually.
  • Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
